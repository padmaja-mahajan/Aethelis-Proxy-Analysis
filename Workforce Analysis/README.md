# Aethelis Proxy Workforce Analysis

## Executive summary

Name of the organization: Aethelis Proxy

The report shows who works at Aethelis Proxy (grade, role, age, tenure, work mode), how the workforce has grown since 2011, and where it sits.

The findings are for active employees (2,474) as of 30 June 2026, the data extraction date. The 650 former employees are excluded unless stated.

## Key findings

#### 1. Scale and growth

- The workforce grew from 33 employees in 2011 to 2,474 active employees as of 30 June 2026.
- Net additions (year-end headcount) have held at about 250-290 a year (254, 260, 249 and 290 for 2018-2021). Percentage growth has slowed from 68% in 2016 to about 19% by 2020-21, because the base is larger.
- Hires peaked at 353 in 2024 and 342 in 2025, with 82 and 65 exits in those years. Hires and exits include former employees.
- Exits peaked at 75 in 2020 and eased to 62 in 2021, with no sustained exodus during the COVID period. Hiring continued at a steady pace, at 298 in 2020 and 253 in 2021.
- The trend line points to roughly 3.4K active employees by 2036. The confidence range is very wide (about 1K to 5.8K), so treat it as directional.

#### 2. Business unit mix

Product & Technology has 820 employees (33%), followed by Corporate (381), Corporate Services (358), Customer (322), Trust & Governance (299) and Revenue (294).

#### 3. Job and leadership mix

- Technical (902) and Operations (443) make up 54% of the workforce.
- 54% are individual contributors (1,343). The rest are team leads (514), managers (351), directors (204) and executives (62).
- The grade structure is an hourglass, with 605 at G1, 74 at G4, 568 at G5 and 62 at G8.
- Position criticality is Low 48%, High 40.5%, Medium 9% and Critical 2.5%.

#### 4. Demographics and work profile

- Young workforce. The average age is 30 (range 21-55). About 82% are 35 or under. About 4% are over 45, and a further 5% are aged 41-45.
- Tenure. The average is 5.8 years and the maximum is 15.2, consistent with a company founded in 2011. The minimum is 0.04 years, reflecting recent hires. By band, about 10% have under 1 year, 22% have 1-2 years, 25% have 3-5 years, 35% have 6-10 years and 8% have 11-15 years.
- Employment type. 80% are permanent full-time, 19% fixed-term and 1% graduate trainees.
- Work mode. 78% hybrid, 15% on-site, 6% field-based and 1% remote.
- Gender. 58% male, 41% female and 1% transgender.
- Career track. The split is about 55% individual contributor and 45% management.
- Nationality. 97.4% Indian, consistent with the geography page.

## Recommendations

- Succession and experience depth. A young, mid-tenure workforce with a thin G4 layer and few people over 45 puts pressure on G5-G6 to supply future leaders. Check promotion flow and retention at G3-G5.
- Role definitions. Define "leadership level" clearly, so it isn't confused with people managers. About 226 employees have direct reports, while 1,131 sit in leadership levels above individual contributor. The two figures measure different things.
- Hiring plan. Plan around net additions of about 250-290 a year rather than growth percentages, and confirm whether the 2024-25 pace is intended to continue.
- Representation. Add gender by grade and leadership level to see whether the 41% female share holds in senior layers.
- Workplace policy. With 78% hybrid, review office capacity and policy at the Bengaluru HQ.

## Before you open the report

- Year-end headcount is calculated from hire and termination dates, so it does not depend on the Employment Status slicer. The hires and exits chart also ignores that slicer.
